    --open https://fly.io/apps/nus \
    "prod is green · v0.0.3-preview.2"
How it reads. One list of what came in, newest first, “to you” marked in signal. Read it, open it in a tab, put it off, or send it to the phone. Other tabs’ rows don’t change. The Desk’s own tab and the drawer count what’s to you.
How it works. Workers on the existing tokio runtime, one per source. Conditional GETs (ETag, 304, GitHub’s X-Poll-Interval), JMAP over EventSource, IMAP IDLE re-armed every 29 min. Items in profile/desk.db, sealed by the vault, tokens in the OS keychain. Private windows have no Desk.
For. Costs almost nothing: no renderer, no RAM per source. Exact counts, real read state, and it works when nus has no tabs at all. Ready for the phone page and MCP.
Against. Setup per source, and it can’t reach the two biggest inboxes without OAuth review. It also copies part of the sites’ own UI, so it has to stay smaller than them.
