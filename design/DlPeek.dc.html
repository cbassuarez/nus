To build. A partial sidebar draw (only the foot), a live download row with its own hits (pause, show in folder), a hold timer that pauses on hover, and a “first few times” counter in prefs. The most work, about two days.
How it reads. Click, and the bottom of the sidebar slides in under the link’s side of the window: the same six icons you’d see if you revealed it, with downloads lit and the file sitting on it. You can act on it right there. Leave it alone and it goes back after about three seconds; hover it and it stays.
Motion. The foot slides on the sidebar’s own reveal, Motion::travel(SIDEBAR), so it’s the reveal you already know, only shorter. The row slides down 10 px on ROW, the icon gets one Bob, and the hold is 2.9 s (paused while the pointer is over it or the key window is elsewhere).
For. It shows the real control in its real place, so the lesson is exact, and it’s clickable at the moment you care (open, pause, show in folder). Works for tiny files. No stand-ins.
Against. The interface moves without being asked, which goes against “the sidebar hides.” Three seconds is long beside a click, and it covers the page’s bottom-left. It gets old by the tenth download, so it wants a rule like “only the first few times.”
