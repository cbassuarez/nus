B · Dwell

Time. Position says only between (a rule) or on (a row). Resting on a row escalates: after 450ms it springs to stack, after another 450ms to join, shown as a fill along the row and a verb flip. Folded stacks and the window name are spring-loaded too, so a tab can be carried into another window and placed there with the same gesture (macOS spring-loaded folders, Vivaldi/Firefox/Edge drag-to-group). Forgiving and sparse; the cost is waiting, and the timer only starts when the pointer has nearly stopped.
