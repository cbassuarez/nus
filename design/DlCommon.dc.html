reduced motion · the whole of it

The state appears in place. No slip, no rule run, no peek.
Only when you did it. Motion plays only when download_origin is Some, meaning your click, your key or a nus action. Background and page-started downloads update the icon silently. It’s the same test that already decides whether a download may keep the machine awake.
Once per gesture. Three or more files within 600 ms are one movement labelled “3 files”. Repeats of the same URL within 5 s are not shown again.
Reduced motion. Nothing travels. The notch or icon state appears with one 80 ms tint, and the tooltip carries the words. The register scales every duration as it does today (Motion::travel clamps to 0.16–0.44 s).
Sound. Two new events on the sound page: download.start (quiet by default) and download.done → ready, played only when the download took over 5 s, like page.ready.
The icon becomes a dial. Today the foot icon turns signal with a full underline while anything is active. Instead the underline fills by bytes, and the done dot is the one the menu drawer already uses. Words live in the tooltip, never in caps on the row.

research

Safari. The file’s icon arcs from the click to the Downloads stack in the Dock, or to the toolbar button. When the stack isn’t in the Dock, people report the icon stuck on screen. Lesson: never fly to a place that isn’t drawn, which is why the hidden sidebar gets a notch.
Chrome (2023). Replaced the bottom shelf with a toolbar button that animates progress, and opens its bubble when a download completes. Lesson: progress on the button is right; opening a panel unasked is loud (C’s risk).
Firefox. An animated arrow drops into the toolbar button. Support threads ask how to turn it off. Lesson: once, short, and under the motion setting.
nus today. The Downloads icon goes signal with a full underline while active. IconMotion::Bob exists “like a download landing” but downloads never use it. Save as PDF raises a toast.
