compact · the cell becomes a corner

At 48px the cell can't fit, so it keeps its shape as an 8px corner mark: filled signal waits, hatched failed, ink is busy, an outline is unseen. The words go to the tooltip.
How it reads. Down the right edge is one column that always means the same thing: what the tab is doing now. Your eye runs down it like a timetable. The second line is where the tab is (folder and branch, host, site). Nothing moves; the clock ticks once a second. Only "waits" fills with signal, so the one thing that needs you is the one loud cell.

Stacks. Folded, the second line is a spine: a tick per member, ink for the one you were on, signal for one that waits; the cell rolls up the stack's loudest state ("1 waits").

Cost. Every row is 40px, not 30: fewer tabs fit (17 at 800px, against 24).
