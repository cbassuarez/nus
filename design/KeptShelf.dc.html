How it reads. Folders become the shelf: everything you keep sits under the tabs, in collections that fold. This project comes first when the window is bound to a folder and holds that repo's links, ports and notes. To read is a collection that empties itself as you finish, with a meter where a count would be. Live folders (GitHub, ports, rules) sit in the same list, marked by their own icon rather than the ribbon. Drag a tab onto a collection to keep it there; drag a kept row onto the page to open it.
For. Everything kept is one glance away, in the place your hand already goes. It's the closest to how Folders and the reading-list pin work today, so it's the least new.
Against. The sidebar is already busy: pins, tabs, ledger lines, stacks. 341 items don't fit, so the shelf is really a few open collections, and the rest still needs somewhere to go. A hover sidebar hides it exactly when you aren't looking.
Pinned stays the top grid; a pin is just a kept item with a place in it. Unpinning leaves the item kept.
