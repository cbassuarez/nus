C · Director

An explicit verb. The drag carries a verb and a director bar rises along the window's foot listing every verb and its key, the live one lit. No key: move (reorder, depth by lane, page quarters tile). Hold shift: join, and every target re-skins as a plug. Hold ctrl: copy. Press 1–9: send to that window (shelf), N a new one. Keys can change mid-drag; release decides. Unambiguous, and the only one where sending to another window on Wayland needs no pointer position. The cost is a second hand.
