Show, don't describe. nus already draws every pane as a texture (the floating player uses it), so a row can carry a proof: a 50px live crop of the tab itself. Three tabs get one at rest: the one you're on, any that wait for you, and any that failed and you haven't seen. Everything else stays a single 30px row with a quiet word at its end.

Hover opens a card: a bigger live view on top, then plain facts (what runs, for how long, where, last time, memory).

Stacks show a filmstrip of members when one of them is active, and a contact sheet on hover.

Cost. Proofs are textures: the GPU memory of three crops, and a redraw when they change. The sidebar's height changes as tabs start and stop waiting, and rows move under the pointer. Proofs only grow while the pointer is outside the sidebar.
