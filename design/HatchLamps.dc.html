B · Lamps

The sheet, rolled up. A small tab hangs from the top edge, cut from the hatch’s own paper with its edges and its lip. Inside is one lamp per piece of work, drawn with the open hatch’s row marks and the blocks’ lamps. A square breathes while something runs and fills from the bottom with progress. A diamond means it needs you, a circle means it finished, and a hatched square means it failed. Every lamp is a button: its tooltip says what it is, and a click brings that session down.

Vermilion · over a light browser
The loop. 0 s: two lamps, one filling and one breathing. 3 s: a diamond joins for fly deploy. 6 s: cargo test becomes a circle, blooms, and the tab lengthens with its title. 8.8 s: you reach for the diamond.
How it reads. Count the lamps to see how much is up there, and read their shapes for what each is doing. Shape carries the state, so it reads the same in Blueprint, whose signal is white like its text, as in a theme with a red signal. When ready, the tab shrinks to a chevron, or to hollow squares for shells left up there.
Motion. Running squares breathe (opacity 1 → .35 over 2.4 s, all in phase so they read as one thing), and a diamond breathes the same way. A notice blooms the circle once (a ring out to 2.6×, 0.5 s) and lengthens the tab on ROW. Lamps keep their places; new work joins on the right. Under reduced motion nothing breathes.
Footprint. 19 px tall at rest (13 when ready). 26 px wide when ready, about 105 px with six lamps and the ⋯. The hit area is the tab.
For. Exact at a glance, with no words: the count and each state. It uses the open hatch’s own marks, so the closed and open hatch agree. Each lamp is a target, which saves a step when you know which session you want. It follows “stateful icons with tooltips”, and it’s the cheapest to build.
Against. It’s a widget more than an ornament: a box over whatever is at the top of the screen (here, the active browser tab). It grows with your work, it’s the least beautiful of the three, and 7 px lamps are tight targets for touch.
To build. Lamps in hatch_badge_frame (rects and a rotated rect). Hover and tooltips on the badge window. Hit::Job per lamp, as the notice click already does. The notice’s second row. About a day.
