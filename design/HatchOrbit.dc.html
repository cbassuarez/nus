C · Orbit

The mark, keeping time. The icon’s band, the open, tapered orbit tilted −24°, hangs from the top edge on a 1 px cord, without its n. As on the plate, places are stops on the band, and here the stops are your work. While anything runs the band turns, one lap every 9 s. When something needs you, the orbit stops and that stop comes to the front. A finished stop turns round, and a failed one drops off and hangs below. Reach for it and it turns to face you; pull the cord and the hatch comes down.

The loop. 0 s: the band turns with two stops, one square filling. 3 s: fly deploy needs input, so the band eases to a stop and a diamond sits at the front. 6 s: cargo test turns round and its tag hangs. 8.8 s: you reach for it.
How it reads. If the band is turning, something is working. If it’s still, either it’s waiting for you or there’s nothing to do, and the stops say which. It’s the most recognisable thing nus could put on a desktop, because it’s the icon. Idle, it’s the still band alone, something the eye can rest on.
Motion. One lap every 9 s, linear: the tapered belly travels and the stops stay put, so nothing jumps. Stopping for you eases out over 0.8 s. Hover tilts it to face you on ROW. Under reduced motion the band never turns, and running stops breathe instead, as in B.
Footprint. 64 × 30 px, hanging about 5 px below the edge on its cord, plus a tag during a notice. It needs a see-through window; without one it sits on a 72 × 34 paper plate. The hit area is the orbit’s box.
For. The most beautiful, and unmistakably nus. A turning band is calm, periodic motion, like a clock’s sweep hand: it says “working” without a number, and stopping is a gentle way to ask for you. The cord gives “pull to open” a physical shape.
Against. Ornament first. Motion in the corner of the eye always costs a little attention, so it has to stay slow and stop when idle. The stops are 5 px targets on a 64 px ellipse, and past six they crowd. The round, drawn mark breaks “radii 0”, as the icon already does.
To build. A band primitive: an SDF ellipse arc with a taper, or the band baked per frame the way dock_icon bakes the icon. Stops, the cord, hover and drag on the badge window, and the paper-plate fallback. About two and a half days.
