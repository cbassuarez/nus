when it shows · unchanged

It shows when Compact work status is on and something is running, needs you or finished unseen, or when nus is in the background (the case that reads “Hatch · ready” today), or during a 6 s Completion notice. It’s hidden while the hatch is open and never takes focus (show_passive). A click opens Work, or during a notice, that session.

hollow · an idle shell up there (B only). Status::Idle.

rules

  1. Dark when normal. With nothing to say and nus in front, draw nothing. With nus in the background, draw only the smallest rung.
  2. Shape first, then motion, then colour. Blueprint’s signal is white, the same as its text, so colour is never the only carrier.
  3. Words only when you look: tooltips on hover and the title in a notice, and the only words are yours (the session’s title).
  4. At most one slow loop (2.4 s or longer), all in phase, and nothing moves when nothing runs. Reduce Motion keeps the state and drops the travel.
  5. Thin and polite. 19 px or less at rest. Take clicks only on what’s drawn. Hide over fullscreen windows and while the hatch is open, and never take focus.
  6. Marks keep their places; new work joins at the right. Sorting by urgency belongs to Work, not the closed hatch.
  7. A piece of the theme’s paper with an ink keyline, so it reads on light and dark desktops alike.
  8. Accessible: the badge exposes the summary (“2 running · 1 needs input”) as its label, and each mark is a button.
