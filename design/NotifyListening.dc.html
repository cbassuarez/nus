How it reads. Gmail is a row with 12 in its cell. Slack turns signal when someone writes to you, and the second line is what they said until you look. Nothing to set up: open the page, say keep listening.
How it works. CEF has no Notification API, so nus defines one: Notification, showNotification and setAppBadge call a nusNotify binding, injected the same way the video and print scripts are. Titles like “(14)” and “*” are read as counts. A listening tab stays lifecycle-active (no 30-minute sleep, no once-a-minute timers) and paints nothing while hidden.
For. Works on any site on day one, including ones nobody wrote code for. Costs nothing until you listen. Reuses the cell, the ladder and the away rows as they are.
Against. Roughly 150–400 MB for each listening page, so it’s capped at 4. It only knows what the page says: a signed-out page goes stale (dashed), and a page that never calls Notification only shows a count.
